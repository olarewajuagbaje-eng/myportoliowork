# Compact project card spacing

## Goal
Remove the large empty areas inside project cards on phones and larger screens while keeping every project and its content unchanged.

## Changes
- Let each project card size itself to its actual content instead of matching the tallest card.
- Keep the action buttons directly after the card content rather than forcing them to the bottom of an oversized card.
- Preserve the carousel, filters, swipe controls, project ordering, and case-study actions.
- Verify the featured projects on a phone-sized screen and confirm cards no longer contain excessive blank space.
